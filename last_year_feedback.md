# VP Promotion — Last Year's Panel Feedback (2025)

## Result: Rejected

## Summary
Needs stronger depth of impact and challenges overcome. Sponsors and firmwide contribution not visibly present.

## Positives
- 25M saving example underpinned by auto association/dissociation — good
- 100s of analyst hours saved sounds great
- gRPC valued, contribution to late-night release support noted
- Panel valued strong technical ability — great for CTO panel
- Good opportunity to show leadership
- Good examples of CSR and Engineering activities

## Critical Issues

| Area | Feedback |
|------|----------|
| **Impact** | Comes across as strong technically but needs to articulate business impact in simpler terms |
| **Platform** | Show leadership, emphasize management leadership and cooperation, partnership — not just technical |
| **Saving 25M** | Big saving, good example — but explain what this is, don't use tech jargon. People may not know acronyms |
| **gRPC** | Good proof of concepts — but explain what these benefit to the business |
| **Behaviour** | Always bring back your activities/examples to the business benefit they provide |
| **Cooperation** | Need to show how you cooperate with others on top of technical problem solving — emphasize solving complex real problems |
| **Firmwide + DA** | Good examples but need to show how you lead in these |
| **Sponsorship** | Not critical but good to have — all others will have them. Get 1 from tech + 1 outside CLM |
| **PPT** | Text too small to read. Avoid acronyms. Not everyone familiar with subject matter |
| **D4 involvement** | Need more D4 (Director-level) involvement shown |
| **Individual contribution** | Couldn't illustrate strong individual contribution in solution design |
| **Firmwide** | Weak in firmwide contribution. No sponsors |

## Key Themes to Fix This Year

1. **Tell business stories, not tech stories** — translate everything to business value
2. **Show leadership & cooperation**, not just technical ability
3. **Simplify language** — no jargon, no acronyms, explain like panel is non-technical
4. **Get sponsors** — ideally 1 from tech + 1 outside CLM
5. **Firmwide contribution** — needs to be much stronger and visible
6. **PPT must be readable** — bigger text, multiple slides, clean design
7. **Show solution design ownership** — not just delivery
8. **D4 involvement** — highlight Director-level engagement
