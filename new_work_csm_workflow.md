# New Work — CSM Identification & Auto-Answering Agentic Workflow

## Overview
First ever agentic workflow in Deutsche Bank Client Life Cycle Management (dbCLM). Built using the Nexus AI framework. Automates the identification of Client Senior Managers (CSMs) from documents and auto-answers CSM-related questions.

## Business Context
- **CSM** = Client Senior Managers
- **dbCLM** = Deutsche Bank Client Life Cycle Management

## Before & After

### Before (Manual Process)
- Analysts had to manually read through documents to find and identify Client Senior Managers
- Time-consuming, error-prone, manual effort for every document

### After (Agentic Workflow)
- AI-powered workflow automatically reads documents, identifies CSMs, and auto-answers CSM questions
- Eliminates manual document reading for CSM identification
- Just released — metrics expected in ~1 month

## Significance
- **First agentic workflow in the entire dbCLM business unit**
- Proves Nexus AI framework works in production — not just a framework, but a real business solution delivered
- Pioneering AI adoption for the business unit
- Demonstrates end-to-end ownership: built the framework (Nexus AI) AND delivered the first production use case on top of it
