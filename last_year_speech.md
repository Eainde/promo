I work on Data and Doc platform of Client Life Cycle Management at Deutsche Bank. Our platform is responsible for how client documents and data are stored, interpreted, onboarding and automation journey. It's the heart of compliance.

One example, I designed, developed auto associations and de-associations of documents. These features intelligently attach uploaded documents to right questions, saving analysts from doing it manually.
