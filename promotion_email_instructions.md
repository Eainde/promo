# 2026 Promotion Process — AVP→VP / CLM Tech

**From:** Peter Salerno (peter.salerno@db.com)
**Audience:** Group of CLM Tech AVPs in 2026 promotion process

## Key Dates
- **Pitch:** Wednesday **20-May-2026** (meeting invite to follow)
- **Form due:** Tuesday morning **19-May-2026** — sent to Peter Salerno

## Pitch Format
- **7-minute verbal pitch** to CLM Tech management team
- Format comparable to RCP and Global promotion processes
- Delivered via Teams/video — troubleshoot tech issues in advance
- Show operating at VP level and ready to progress to later phases
- Deliver as if presenting to **final global panel** — assume **no prior knowledge** of your work
- Even if you know CLM panelists, present as a finalist to a diverse panel from around tech
- **DO NOT assume any prior understanding**
- **Avoid three letter acronyms (TLAs)**
- Talk as if impressing a friend who **does not work at Deutsche Bank**
- "If they shrug and go 'so what?' — chances are so will the panel"

## Slide Allowance
- **CTO/Engineering track candidates: 1-page presentation** (Akshay's track)
- CIO candidates: up to 4-page PowerPoint

## Promotion Form
- Document shared first with CLM Tech panelists, later with RCP and Global panelists
- Tell a story showing your platform and impact are VP caliber and why firm needs you as VP
- YOUR job to make panel crystal clear on why YOU should be promoted
- Panel looks for **strong EVIDENCE**
- Must convince an audience who has **never met you** and is **not familiar with CLM or KYC**
- Provide examples and specific contributions
- Show execution of Leadership Kompass
- **CTO candidates:** use "2022 Promotion Nomination Form Eng (VP)" doc
- CIOs may use "Promotion Form Template - blank.doc"
- People managers: use form to evidence your work

## Pitch Rating Criteria (1-5 scale, 5 dimensions)

### 1. Role/Platform
- Succinctly say what you do and why
- What opportunity or problem are you solving for the bank?
- Engineering context, business importance, team size, programme alignment
- Range of technologies used and how it adds to overall experience

### 2. Career Deliverables / Individual Impact & Achievements ⭐ CONCENTRATE HERE MOST
- Strength of individual impact to the organisation in terms of deliveries
- **"We recommend you concentrate on this area most in your presentation"**
- Examples of work and clarity on **specific contributions**
- CTO/Engineering track: **speak to deliverables in terms of ECDF behaviours**

### 3. Firmwide Contributions
- Building culture of Deutsche Bank beyond day-to-day project work
- Specific examples including representation and fostering of talent
- Solutions that benefit those outside immediate team
- GEE tasks, Engineering days, diversity initiatives, firmwide initiatives
- **Demonstrate driving re-use**

### 4. Sponsors / Endorsers
- **Minimum 2 sponsors** (not limited to)
- List sponsors and indicate if agreed to sponsor
- Include endorsement statements if possible

### 5. Q&A
- Panel asks questions at end
- **3-5 minutes total**

## Process After Pitch
- Pitch + form → CLM Tech selects candidates → proceed to **RCP review process**
- RCP follows similar process → selects candidates to proceed
- **Limited slots at each stage** — invest time now
